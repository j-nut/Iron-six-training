Routine & exercise variety Your place in the program and when exercises change

The base rotation is Push A → Legs A → Pull A → Push B → Legs B → Pull B. Completed sessions move the program forward; rest days keep your place.

A/B sessions change the emphasis. Accessories offer variety while benchmark lifts stay consistent so progress is measurable. Your current training outlook shows the next session for your profile.

Weights, reps & progression How your actual performance informs the next recommendation

Starting estimates use your profile, exercise and equipment limits. Once you log a movement, actual weight, reps and reps in reserve take priority.

More reps with capacity left can raise the next recommendation. Missed reps or reaching failure can lower it. Log what you actually do to keep future suggestions useful.
