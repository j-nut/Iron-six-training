Why this exists

I'm a dad with a job. For a few years that was my whole excuse, and it was a good one — right up until I couldn't get through a Saturday at the park without needing to sit down.

It hadn't happened all at once. It never does. A missed session here, a long week there, and one day the person in the photo didn't look like the person in the mirror.

So I made one deal with myself: iron, six days a week. No heroics, no perfect plan — just turn up six times and let the rest sort itself out.

The turning up was the easy part. The apps were not. One wanted ninety minutes I didn't have. One wanted a cable machine I'd never own. Most wanted me to build my own programme first, which is a strange thing to ask of the exact person who doesn't yet know what they're doing.

So I built the thing I actually needed. Twenty minutes before the school run, with two dumbbells and a bench, is a real workout — and it should be programmed like one, not treated as a failed version of somebody's ideal hour.

That's Iron Six. Named after the deal.

What it does that others don't

Every one of these is in the app today — not a roadmap.

Time-aware

15 minutes is a session, not a failure

Change the time and the workout is rebuilt around priorities. It drops the accessory work and keeps the movements that matter — instead of cutting the end off an hour-long plan.

Equipment-aware

It only ever programmes what you own

Pick from a catalogue or type in anything. Add a kettlebell and the library grows to match it. A coverage score shows which movements your setup can't train yet, and what would fix it.

Adaptive

Your numbers come from your lifting

Log weight, reps and how much you had left. The next suggestion moves from that — not from a formula based on your body weight and a guess.

Focus

One exercise on screen at a time

The session opens on setup, then walks one movement at a time and remembers where you were when you come back. Full form illustrations, big enough to actually read mid-set.

Coach

An AI that can see your workout

It knows today's session, your history and your equipment. Ask why a weight was suggested, swap a movement, or get warm-up sets. It never invents equipment you don't have.

Yours

Offline, exportable, multi-person

Every set is written down locally first and survives a dead signal. Export a full backup whenever you like and restore it anywhere. One account can hold several people's training.
