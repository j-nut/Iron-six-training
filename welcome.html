Why I built it

I'm a dad with a full-time job. I stopped training properly around the time the kids arrived, and didn't really notice for a couple of years.

Turning up again was the easy part. The apps weren't. One assumed I had ninety minutes. One kept giving me cable machines I don't own. Most wanted me to write my own programme first, which is hard when you don't know what you're doing yet.

What worked was short sessions, six days a week — twenty or thirty minutes before everyone else was up. It was the only thing that fit around work and kids.

So I built this. It treats a short session with limited kit as a real workout and programmes it properly. The name is just that: iron, six days a week.

What it does

All of this is in the app now.

Time

It resizes to the time you have

Set 15, 30 or 60 minutes and the session is rebuilt around what matters most. It drops accessory work first and keeps the main lifts, rather than cutting the end off a longer plan.

Equipment

It only uses equipment you have

Pick from a list or type in whatever you own. Add a kettlebell and the exercise library grows to match. It also shows which movements your setup can't cover yet, and what would fix that.

Progression

Weights come from your own lifts

Log the weight, the reps, and roughly how many you had left. The next session starts from that, not from a formula based on your body weight.

In the gym

One exercise at a time

You see the exercise you're on, not a list of twenty sets. Put your phone down between sets and it's still there when you pick it up. Every exercise has a form guide you can actually read.

Coach

A coach that knows your session

Ask why a weight was suggested, swap an exercise, or get warm-up sets. It can see today's workout, your history and your equipment, so it won't suggest kit you don't have.

Your data

Your data, and more than one of you

Sets are saved on your phone first, so a dead signal doesn't lose anything. You can export a full backup any time and restore it later. One account can hold more than one person's training.

What it costs

Nothing at the moment. Iron Six is early, and everything in it is free while it is. If there's ever a paid tier it'll be cheap, and anything you've already logged stays yours and stays exportable either way.
